# Privacy Policy

Article from Notes is a set of written instructions (a skill) for Claude and other AI assistants. It contains no code and runs no programs of its own.

## What we collect

Nothing. The author of Article from Notes does not collect, store, receive or share any of your data, including your notes, articles or usage.

## Where your data goes

Your notes and articles are processed by the AI assistant you use the skill in, such as Claude. That provider's own privacy policy applies, for example [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

If web search is turned on in your assistant, the assistant may search the web to find facts for your article. Those searches are made by your assistant under its provider's terms, not by Article from Notes.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/Occamic/article-from-notes/issues
