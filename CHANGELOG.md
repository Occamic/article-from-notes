# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (first public release)

- Turns rough notes into a blog or magazine article in four stages: organise, outline, draft, polish.
- Settings for tone, length, audience and structure, an optional style sample, and optional social posts.
- Publishing details: alternative headlines, SEO title, meta description, slug, tags and excerpt.
- Honesty rules: added facts are cited (when web search is available) or marked `[check]`, and every addition is listed under "Added material". Anecdotes, quotes and results for the writer are never invented.
- Expands thin notes honestly to reach the target length, and asks a few questions first when the notes are very short.
- Every reply ends with a version line (`article-from-notes v1.0`) so you can see the skill ran.
