4 day week trial - notes for blog
- ran trial jan-jun 2026, team of 12 (design + dev)
- fridays off, same pay, no extra hours mon-thu (tried 10hr days at first, dropped after 3 wks - people exhausted)
- output: shipped 14 client projects vs 13 same period last yr
- sick days down from 31 to 18
- two people struggled - client-facing roles, clients expected fri replies
- fix: rota for account managers, one covers fri, gets mon off instead
- meetings cut: standup now async in slack, weekly all-hands 60->30 min
- Priya (ops lead): "the meetings were the real problem, not the hours"
- recruitment: 3x applicants for last job ad vs before
- sick days fell a lot (31->18)
- decided to keep it permanently from july
- cost? didn't really measure - maybe mention overtime?
- tools: moved to async updates, shared calendar showing who's off
